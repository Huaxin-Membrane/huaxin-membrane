Company

A Tsinghua PhD team, building membranes to industrial scale.

HUAXIN Membrane is the brand of Huaxin Nano (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd. It was incubated by Tsinghua x-lab, Tsinghua Tongfang and Tsinghua Chuang+, and founded in 2023 by three Tsinghua University PhDs together with R&D partners in Europe, the Middle East and beyond.

Mission

Advance the localisation and global application of high-end separation membranes. For water treatment, energy and chemicals, from China to the Middle East and beyond.

History

From research to 10 million m² a year.

  1. Before 2023

    Decades of research

    The founding team's membrane research at Tsinghua University and with partner institutions.

  2. 2023

    Brand founded, mass production

    HUAXIN Membrane launched; large-scale production of full-category RO/NF products.

  3. 2023–2025

    Long-term field use

    Mine-water reuse and ZLD plants in Ningxia; groundwater tests in the Middle East.

  4. 2024–2025

    Scale-up in Jining

    Shandong Jinxin Membrane Technology established with a 10M m²/yr sheet line and independent IP.
