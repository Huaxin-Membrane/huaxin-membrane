ZLD

Zero liquid discharge

Industrial wastewater bound for zero liquid discharge is high in salinity and hardness, foul-prone, and needs frequent aggressive cleaning. Membranes that lose rejection or foul quickly push more load onto evaporators and crystallisers.

The membrane's role

RO concentrates the stream before thermal treatment. Elements here are chosen for fouling resistance, cleaning tolerance and oxidation resistance, not just day-one rejection.

Design considerations

  • Feed TDS, hardness and sulfate drive scaling risk
  • Cleaning frequency and chemistry (pH 1–13 short-term for HUAXIN RO)
  • Pretreatment and SDI ≤ 5 at the element

DW

High-quality drinking water

Municipal surface and well water can carry hardness, colour, odour and trace micropollutants, while full demineralisation strips minerals that make water taste good.

The membrane's role

Nanofiltration softens and removes organics and micropollutants while retaining part of the beneficial minerals; UF upstream removes particles and microorganisms.

Design considerations

  • Target hardness and mineral retention
  • Local potable-water certification requirements (not stated in the manual; ask HUAXIN)
  • UF or other pretreatment ahead of NF

REUSE

Municipal wastewater reuse

Reclaimed municipal water varies in quality and carries organics and biological load that foul downstream membranes.

The membrane's role

MBR replaces clarification with membrane separation; UF and anti-fouling RO polish the effluent for reuse.

Design considerations

  • Biological load and MBR sizing (150–400 m³/d per HUAXIN module)
  • Fouling potential at the RO stage
  • Energy use and flux targets

SEA

Seawater desalination

Seawater is high in salinity and biofouling potential; boron rejection and stable long-term performance matter as much as initial numbers.

The membrane's role

Seawater RO elements carry the desalination stage; the same elements can be used where high-value by-products are extracted from concentrate.

Design considerations

  • Feed salinity, temperature and boron targets
  • Biofouling control and pretreatment
  • Operating pressure up to 8.0 MPa for HX-SW

Long-term operation: Mine-water reuse, Phase II RO — fouling

Li

Lithium extraction from salt lakes

Salt-lake brines differ widely in composition; separating lithium from magnesium and other ions is the core difficulty.

The membrane's role

Selective nanofiltration and RO are customised to the brine to concentrate lithium and reduce reliance on adsorbents or auxiliary agents.

Design considerations

  • Mg/Li ratio and brine composition
  • Customised membranes are developed per project
  • Pilot testing before full design

UPW

High-purity water for industry

Semiconductor, medical and food production need consistently pure water at low energy cost.

The membrane's role

Low- and extra-low-pressure RO delivers high salt rejection and flux as the core of multi-stage purification.

Design considerations

  • Second-pass RO and polishing steps
  • Feed below 1,000–2,000 ppm for XLP / LP
  • Energy per m³ at design flux
