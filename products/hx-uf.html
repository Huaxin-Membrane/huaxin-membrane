Overview

Column Ultrafiltration (UF) Module

  • Hollow-fiber structure: high specific surface area improves flux
  • PVDF membrane material with excellent chemical resistance
  • Wide operating window: pH 1–14 and 5–45 °C
  • Full-flow or cross-flow filtration
