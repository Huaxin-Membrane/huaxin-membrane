Overview

Extra Low Pressure RO Element

  • High permeate flow at ultra-low operating pressure
  • Significantly reduces energy and system operating costs
  • Stable salt rejection of 99.0% at 500 ppm NaCl and 100 psi (0.69 MPa)
  • Three models across 4040 and 8040 element formats

Technical specifications

Operating limits

Design within these limits.

Exceeding operating limits can shorten element life. Confirm system design with HUAXIN technical support.

Maximum operating pressure4.1 MPa
Maximum feed flow rate16 gpm (3.6 m³/h) for 4040 · 75 gpm (17.0 m³/h) for 8040
Maximum feed water temperature45 °C
Maximum feed SDI (silt density index)5
Maximum free chlorine in feed< 0.1 ppm
Continuous operating pH range2–11
pH range, short-term cleaning (30 min)1–13
Maximum pressure drop per element15 psi (0.10 MPa)
