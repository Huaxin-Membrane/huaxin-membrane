Manufacturing

Made in Jining, by the people who designed it.

HUAXIN's founders built a fully independent production line covering raw materials, equipment and process. In 2025 the team established Shandong Jinxin Membrane Technology Development Co., Ltd. in Jining with 10 million m² of annual membrane-sheet capacity.

Process control

Thousands of parameters. The few that matter.

Both the base membrane and the functional layer depend on manufacturing conditions. HUAXIN combined computer simulation with extensive process experiments to find the parameters that drive performance and stability at scale.

Continuous optimisation of key equipment components improved the precision of the slit-extrusion process, raising the stability and yield of the line and supporting scalable production of high-end membrane formulations.
