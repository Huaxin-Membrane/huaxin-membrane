Executive Summary

Membranes engineered at the molecular scale, proven in the field.

Huaxin Nano is a technology-driven enterprise focused on high-end separation membranes. Its mission is to break foreign technological monopolies and advance the localization and global application of membrane materials.

The HUAXIN Membrane brand was incubated by Tsinghua x-lab, Tsinghua Tongfang and Tsinghua Chuang+, and brings a Tsinghua University doctoral team together with leading R&D institutions in Europe, the Middle East and beyond. Its “molecule–nano–composite membrane” hierarchical design is positioned to deliver better selectivity, stability and permeability than leading international products, at a competitive price.

The portfolio covers the full water-treatment chain. Reverse osmosis ranges from extra-low-pressure HX-XLP and low-pressure HX-LP through brackish water HX-BW and anti-fouling HX-BWFR to seawater HX-SW. Nanofiltration includes the drinking-water HX-HQ and the industrial salt-separation HX-BM. The line is completed by the HX-MBR membrane bioreactor and the HX-UF column ultrafiltration module.

Field results from mine-water reuse, zero liquid discharge and Middle Eastern groundwater projects are collected in Chapter 05.

About HUAXIN

A Tsinghua-born, technology-driven membrane company with its own raw-material, equipment and process know-how.

All three co-founders hold Tsinghua University PhDs. Drawing on decades of R&D experience, they built a fully independent production line that integrates raw materials, equipment and processes.

To meet global demand, Huaxin co-established Shandong Jinxin Membrane Technology with Shandong Jinyu, launching a production line with 10 million m² of annual output.

With a global vision, Huaxin aims to become a key player in the international high-end separation membrane market, driving sustainable development in water treatment, energy and chemicals.

Brand Introduction

“HUAXIN Membrane” is an international high-end separation membrane brand, incubated jointly by Tsinghua x-lab, Tsinghua Tongfang and Tsinghua Chuang+. It was founded by a doctoral team from Tsinghua University together with top global R&D institutions in Europe, the Middle East and other regions.

Featuring high strength and durability, high desalination rate, high flux and low energy consumption, it serves seawater desalination, zero discharge of high-concentration and complex industrial wastewater, and high-quality drinking water production.

Brand History

Founded in 2023 by a Tsinghua PhD team in partnership with global R&D institutions, HUAXIN Membrane has achieved large-scale production of full-category RO/NF products: seawater desalination (HX-SW), brackish water and anti-fouling (HX-BW, HX-BWFR), high-quality drinking water (HX-HQ) and salt-splitting nanofiltration (HX-BM) membranes. Long-term use in demanding scenarios such as mine-water treatment and industrial wastewater zero discharge has earned strong customer recognition (see Chapter 05). In 2025 the team established Shandong Jinxin Membrane Technology Development Co., Ltd. in Jining, Shandong, and completed a high-end membrane sheet production line with independent intellectual property and 10 million m² of annual capacity.

Chapter 02

Core Technological Advantages

Performance is decided at the molecular scale and secured on the production line.

1

Molecular / Nanoscale Formulation Optimization

Cutting-edge material and product innovation by precisely tuning the molecular or nano structure. This raises the crosslinking degree of the polyamide functional layer, improving oxidation resistance and cleanability.

2

Advanced Membrane Post-Treatment

Delivers exceptional performance and efficiency, ensuring long-term stability and superior functionality of HUAXIN membranes.

3

Comprehensive, Tailored Product Optimization

An integrated membrane–grid–element optimization approach delivers high reliability and quality across application fields, with excellent anti-fouling capability and long-term durability.

From Process Parameters to Scalable Manufacturing

The performance of both the high-performance base membrane and the functional layer is closely tied to the manufacturing process. With thousands of core process parameters in play, identifying the factors that drive product performance and stability in large-scale production is extremely challenging.

By combining computer simulation with extensive process-engineering experiments, the Tsinghua PhD team identified the critical parameters and established accurate correlations between processing conditions and membrane performance. Continuous optimization of key equipment components has significantly improved the precision of the slit extrusion process, raising the operational stability and yield of the production line and supporting scalable manufacturing of high-end membrane formulations.
