Technology

Performance is decided at the molecular scale.

HUAXIN's “molecule–nano–composite” hierarchical design works on three levels: the polyamide chemistry, the treatment of the finished membrane, and the element that carries it.

Three levels of engineering

From molecule to module.

The approach, as stated in HUAXIN's product manual. Specific formulations and process parameters are HUAXIN know-how and are not published.

Molecular and nanoscale formulation

Tuning the molecular and nano structure raises the crosslinking of the polyamide functional layer, improving oxidation resistance and cleanability.

Membrane post-treatment

Post-treatment secures long-term stability. For HX-SW, no oxidative post-treatment is used to boost day-one numbers that fade in service.

Membrane–spacer–element optimisation

Membrane, spacer grid and element are optimised together for each application, targeting fouling resistance and durability.

Process engineering at scale

Simulation plus process experiments identified the critical parameters among thousands, linking processing conditions to membrane performance.

How a spiral-wound element works

Inside every RO and NF element.

Membrane leaves, separated by spacers, are wound around a central tube. Pressure drives water through the membrane; salts stay on the feed side and leave as concentrate.

  • Feed spacerKeeps feed channels open and promotes mixing to limit fouling.
  • Membrane leafPolyamide thin-film layer on a porous support: where separation happens.
  • Permeate carrierCarries purified water spirally toward the centre.
  • Permeate tube and end capsCollect permeate; the spoked end cap holds the wound leaves in place.

Simplified diagram of a generic spiral-wound element, not to scale.

What it means in operation

Built to survive the cleaning cycle.

In industrial reuse and ZLD, membranes are cleaned hard and often. Rejection that holds after aggressive acid and alkaline cleaning is the test that matters.

  • Short-term cleaning tolerance from pH 1 to 13 across HUAXIN RO and NF elements
  • HX-BWFR held about 99.62% rejection after high-intensity alkaline cleaning; the comparison fell below 99%
  • HX-SW gained 0.8 kg over 525 days of operation with repeated cleaning; the comparison gained 22.4 kg
  • High boron rejection and biofouling resistance emphasised for seawater service
